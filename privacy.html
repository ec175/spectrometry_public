Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Spectrometry Shorts ("the App") is a personal automation tool operated by an individual creator (the "Operator") to publish the Operator's own short-form educational videos to the Operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. The App is not offered as a service to the public and does not have third-party end users.

Information We Access

When the Operator connects a social media account, the App receives and stores authorization tokens (OAuth access and refresh tokens) issued by that platform. These tokens are used solely to upload the Operator's own video content and read basic account information (such as the account's channel or user identifier) needed to complete an upload.

The App does not collect, request, or process personal information about any other person. It does not access contacts, messages, viewer data, or analytics about third parties.

How Information Is Used

Access tokens and the Operator's own video files are used only to:

  • Authenticate the Operator with the connected platform;
  • Upload the Operator's own videos (including as drafts) to the Operator's own account;
  • Attach titles, descriptions, and tags that the Operator generates.

Storage and Security

Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on the Operator's own self-hosted, password-protected automation server and are never transmitted to any third party other than the originating platform's official API.

Data Sharing and Retention

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. Tokens are retained only while the corresponding account remains connected and are deleted when the Operator disconnects the account or revokes access from the platform's settings.

Your Rights and Revocation

Because the App acts only on the Operator's own accounts, access can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's app-permissions settings (for example, TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions), which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.
