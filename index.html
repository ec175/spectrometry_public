Clone it, pip install -r requirements.txt, and render. Python 3.10+ and ffmpeg. No LaTeX install needed — the scenes use Pango text rather than MathTex.

The engines

Seven compute something real in numpy and pipe raw frames straight to ffmpeg — no scene graph, no timeline. The eighth makes the publication figures the whole thing grew out of. Each is standalone, catalogued, and shipped with reference stills.

Wind TunnelD2Q9 lattice-Boltzmann and a compressible Navier–Stokes solver with HLLC. Nothing on screen is keyframed.
Field LinesAny closed-form vector field, integrated in constant arclength. Electrostatics, potential flow, magnetostatics.
Lattice GridIlluminated circuit lattices whose content re-rolls on the beat.
Shape PhysicsDiscs, shells and destructible structures — with audio the simulation triggers.
AttractorsForty thousand particles through a chaotic flow. The picture is where they crowd.
OscilloscopeA simulated CRT: phosphor persistence, a film-off-a-screen filter, and a glitch layer.
Chemical ScopeTwenty-nine molecules with FTIR and Raman, drawn on that CRT.
AcademiaStacked simulated spectra for whole compound classes, structures drawn alongside.
Full catalogue — 193 objects, 83 compositions

Every object each engine makes, with parameters, ranges, and notes on what breaks if you change them.
